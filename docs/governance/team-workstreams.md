# Team workstreams

The team has seven members. Names and exact allocations remain to be confirmed. The project owner leads AI, the technical MVP, metrics and the recommendation engine. One other member is examining the customer journey and current technical shortcomings.

| Workstream | Initial ownership | Deliverable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Metrics and evidence architecture | Project owner | Metric definitions, baseline requests, dashboards and experiment measures |
| AI and recommendation MVP | Project owner | Scoped discovery/recommendation prototype and feasibility assessment |
| Journey and technical audit | One teammate, name pending | Current journey map, friction and integration constraints |
| Channels and funnel | Team allocation pending | B2B/D2C channel inventory, AAARRR events and activation points |
| STP and ICPs | Team allocation pending | Evidence-based segment and ICP hypotheses |
| Brand, ritual and cultural relevance | Team allocation pending | Brand fit, respectful cultural insight and motif rationale |
| Commercial recommendation and presentation | Team allocation pending | Prioritized roadmap, economics, narrative and client demonstration |

These are workstreams, not seven assumed individual assignments; members may share workstreams. Record named owners and handoffs when agreed.

## Handoffs
Every handoff should include sources, confidence, unresolved questions and implications for the other workstreams. The journey audit informs activation events and the MVP. ICPs and channels inform campaign experiments. Metrics provide a common decision basis. Brand evidence constrains the voice, ritual framing and thread motif. Maintain a decision record when direction changes.
