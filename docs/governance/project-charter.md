# Project charter

## Purpose
Develop evidence-based, feasible advice on Mauli Rituals' continued relevance, adaptability and commercial sustainability as commerce evolves. This is independent, pro bono academic work for Hult Business School's Future of Commerce course. Professor Nikhil Soi is the instructor attribution supplied by the project owner; it has not been independently verified.

## Scope
- Audit current B2B and D2C channel mix and funnel entry points.
- Define AAARRR events and activation points with explicit measurement requirements.
- Develop STP and in-depth ICP hypotheses grounded in evidence: firmographics for B2B, psychographics and behaviors for D2C.
- Build a metrics vault spanning commercial economics, customer behavior, ritual/cultural engagement and discoverability across SEO, AEO and GEO.
- Explore recommendation and onboarding concepts and a limited technical MVP that supports the marketing recommendation.
- Connect the work through a carefully attributed Kautuka thread motif where supported by brand context.

## Constraints
Retain the existing Shopify platform as the implementation starting point. Do not assume access to private analytics, client budget, APIs, brand permissions or production deployment. Keep proposed voice and dosha experiences exploratory, with transparent limitations and appropriate wellness language.

## Planning horizons
| Horizon | Decision focus | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Immediate | Fix measurable friction and clarify tracking | Current journey audit, channel evidence, baseline availability |
| Short term | Test campaigns and onboarding improvements | Experiment design, segment hypothesis, cost and ownership |
| Long term | Sustain relevance as discovery and commerce evolve | Scenario assumptions, capability dependencies, leading indicators |

Dates and investment amounts are to be set with the team and client context; these horizons are planning categories, not promised delivery schedules.

## Success criteria
Recommendations trace to sources, target audiences and measurable outcomes. Each proposal has an implementation owner, dependencies, cost assumptions, a test and a decision rule. Unknown baselines and speculative capabilities remain visibly unknown. A prototype demonstrates the proposed customer interaction without implying production readiness.
