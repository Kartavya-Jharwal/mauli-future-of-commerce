## Slide 13
11 · THE SENSES
Voice, camera, location: three AEO front doors
Each sense collapses a different micro-moment  and each demands a different 
optimisation
 play 
VOICE  the replenishment sense
Owns I-want-to-do + reorder. 72% of voice shoppers re-buy the same brand; queries average ~29 words vs 4 typed.
Play: conversational content, FAQ schema, featured snippets (40× more likely to be voice answers), Alexa Skills.
CAMERA the discovery sense
VLMs encode style as embeddings: “coastal grandmother” retrieves with zero keyword match. ~20B Google Lens searches/month.
Play: image assets as structured data, semantic alt text (~3× AI visibility), AI-readable brand guidelines (only ~15% of brands have them).
LOCATION the proximity sense
82% of “near me” queries carry intent modifiers; 28% of local voice searches convert same-day.
Play: intent maps over pin accuracy; opt-in is hard architecture (GDPR/CCPA/DPDP), not soft preference.

## Slide 18
16 · SUMMARY
Five takeaways for the answer-engine era
And the bridge back to Thread 04: visibility is no longer bought in auctions — it's earned in consensus.
1 · Clicks were the unit; mentions are
37% of discovery starts in AI interfaces. If the answer doesn't cite you, you were never considered.
2 · Consensus beats optimisation
51.7% of citations go to machine-readable brand sites; independent validation multiplies trust. GEO is earned media at machine speed.
3 · The set expands, the funnel dies
Agents industrialise curation — new entrants get poached in, loyal brands get switched out (37%). Post-purchase excellence now trains the machine gatekeeper.
4 · Loyalty must be an API
Benefits the agent can't query don't exist. Expose points, tiers and member prices — before Visa & Mastercard's rails disintermediate you.
5 · Measure the answer, not the visit
% recommended · share of agent-mind · answer accuracy · zero-regret rate. Attribution ≠ causation — flag every number with its evidence grade.
