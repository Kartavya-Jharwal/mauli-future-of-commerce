## Slide 6
MOVE 02 · READ THE NUMBERS
Partnerships bring the halo; D2C brings thin profit
THE HALO — PARTNERSHIPS
· Bulgari — nine years, bedrooms worldwide
· Lamborghini — from hand sanitiser to a scent for the cars
· Range Rover — found Mauli via a Google search, loved the story
· Emirates amenity shortlist; Stoke Park (Ambani family) asked Mauli to be spa partner
"Nice to have, but not that profitable… no compounding effect for profits."
THE ENGINE — D2C, 61% OF MIX
· The profit engine of the business
· Barely breaks even on first order — discounting plus rising Meta costs
· AOV £87 has to carry acquisition, content, and fulfilment
· US expansion deferred; retail waits until it can be done properly
"We don't want to land and languish."
Retail reality:
 Space NK asks 65% margin + 10–15% marketing contribution + free product + paid placement — economics only large brands can swallow.
Discuss:
 if partnerships don't compound profit, what are they actually worth — and what would you do with them?
