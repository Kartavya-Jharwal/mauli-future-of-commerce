# Live Client Strategic Proposal: Mauli Rituals

**Due:** 2026-10-17 23:59

# **The Brief**

Mauli Rituals is a heritage-driven, premium wellness brand whose equity rests on story, sensory experience, and values, exactly the kind of brand most exposed as discovery and evaluation shift toward AI-personalised, voice, and social/live commerce channels, and exactly the kind that can lead if it gets the strategy right.

Teams will propose how Mauli Rituals should position itself for AI-mediated, voice, social/live, and AR/VR-enabled commerce without diluting the brand's story and sensory identity. The shift has to be woven into positioning, merchandising, pricing, and CX, not bolted on as a channel tactic.

# **Deliverables**

- Written brief — 4 pages maximum (+ uncapped appendix for supporting data or mockups). Reads once, actionable, no filler, no restated brief.

- Presentation — 6-minute team pitch + 4-minute Q&A, maximum 5-8 slides. A compressed oral version of the brief, not a slide-by-slide walkthrough.

# **Required Content**

Both the written brief and the presentation must cover the following:

| **Element** | **What it must contain** |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Recommendation** | One-line strategic recommendation — a decision, not a summary. |
| **2. Diagnosis** | Where Mauli Rituals is exposed to algorithmic/agent intermediation, grounded in specifics from the Week 3 briefing; not generic industry research. |
| **3. Proposal** | Concrete moves across at least two of AI personalisation, voice, social/live commerce, AR/VR each tied to a brand-equity safeguard. |
| **4. Metrics** | What the team would measure to know it worked. |
| **5. Exclusions** | What the team is explicitly not recommending, and why. |

# **Assessed On**

- Strategic judgement and prioritisation  not coverage.

- Specificity to Mauli Rituals over generic frameworks.

- Coherence between the diagnosis and the recommendation.

- Adherence to the brief and pitch format.

- Presentation: clarity and decisiveness under a hard time limit.
