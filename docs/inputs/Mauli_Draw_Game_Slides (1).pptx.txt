## Slide 4
THE METHOD
What you do with your chit 
..
1 · DIG
Read your client pack. What is this problem really about? Who does it hurt?
2 · ASK WHY
Ask “why?” three times to find the real question. Write it
3 · BUILD
Sketch your answer: what it is, who uses it, how it works. Show what you would REFUSE to build.
4 · PITCH
Two minutes: your question → your answer → your refusal. Question first, always.
The facilitator will visit and ask only one thing: “What's the question underneath your chit?”

## Slide 5
CHEAT SHEET · KEEP THIS OPEN ALL SESSION
How to find your answer  Part 1
•  START with the client pack everything important is already in it
•  ASK: Which numbers connect to my chit? What has the founder already tried or refused?
•  ASK WHY three times:  Why is this a problem? → Why? → Why?  The real question hides at the bottom
•  LOOK at maulirituals.com  read one product page word by word. How do they talk? What do they never say?
•  STEAL structure from the brief:  the five non-negotiables, the partner list, the customer numbers use them
•  One strong fact beats ten weak ones

## Slide 6
CHEAT SHEET
How to find your answer  Part 2: the answer builder
•  Every answer needs three parts:  WHAT (one sentence) · WHO + HOW (sketch it) · WHAT WE REFUSE
•  If you can't find data make a sensible guess and SAY it is a guess
•  Check yourself before the pitch:  Does it respect the five non-negotiables?  Did we refuse at least one thing?  Is our best idea on paper, not in someone's head?
STUCK? Do this:
Re-read the founder's quotes in the pack her words usually contain the answer.  ·  Ask the facilitator — but expect a question back.  ·  Still silent after 10 minutes? You haven't found the question yet. Go back to ‘ask why three times.’

## Slide 8
THE PITCH
Two minutes. Three things. Question first.
THE QUESTION
The question your team thinks Mauli should really be asking. Sharp question = half the room won.
THE ANSWER
Your idea — point at the A3 sketch. What it is, who uses it, what it protects.
THE REFUSAL
One thing you would NOT build, and why. Every real strategist refuses something.
BANNED in pitches: follower counts · open rates · “use TikTok” · anything needing a £1m budget

THE FLATTENING FEAR
How do you sell a Mauli ritual through a chatbot without losing the magic?
YOUR TASK:  Design the chatbot experience for someone who has never heard of Ayurveda.
START HERE
Go to maulirituals.com. Read ONE product page word by word.  ·  Ask: what does a customer FEEL that no chatbot can create?  ·  Look at their dosha quiz — it gives value without selling. That is your proof it can be done.

## Slide 11
2
CHIT 2
THE AUDIENCE OR THE NUMBER?
Mauli has 105,000 Instagram followers. How would we know if they actually care?
YOUR TASK:  Show the founder real proof of community — and one idea to build more of it.
START HERE
The founder's own words: “Is she building the audience, or just the numbers?”  ·  Ask: what do people DO when they truly love a brand? (They come back. They finish things. They bring friends.)  ·  Use the pack: 77% open rate, 54% repeat buyers.

## Slide 12
3
CHIT 3
EXPENSIVE ADS
Ads barely break even and Meta is getting worse. How else can Mauli find new customers?
YOUR TASK:  Say where the next marketing pound should go — and what you would stop paying for.
START HERE
Find these numbers in the pack: £87 order, 46% new vs 54% returning, 77% open rate.  ·  Ask: which channel brings buyers who come BACK?  ·  The business already runs on repeat customers — feed that.

## Slide 13
4
CHIT 4
FAMOUS BUT BROKE
Bulgari and Lamborghini love the brand but it brings no profit. How do we fix that?
YOUR TASK:  Turn one partnership moment into a lasting customer.
START HERE
List the partners in the pack: Bulgari, Lamborghini, Range Rover, Emirates, Dishoom.  ·  Ask: what happens AFTER someone tries Mauli at a Bulgari spa? (Usually: nothing.)  ·  Hint: every box already contains a red thread — could something travel with it?

## Slide 14
5
CHIT 5
THE SALE HABIT
Two big 20% sales bring in much of the year's money. How can Mauli earn the same without them?
YOUR TASK:  Design what replaces the sales — without losing the customers who wait for them.
START HERE
Find in the pack: September birthday sale + Black Friday, −20% each.  ·  Ask: why do people wait for the sale? What do they not get the rest of the year?  ·  Look at the Sleep Dharma range — products used every night. What could replace a sale with a habit?

## Slide 15
6
CHIT 6
THE KNOCKING DOOR
Therapists keep asking to buy Mauli and use it on their clients. Build them a simple system.
YOUR TASK:  Design it: who can join, how they buy, how the brand stays protected.
START HERE
The founder said: “We will do it — it's a question of when.” Your job is the HOW.  ·  Ask: what makes a therapist trustworthy enough to represent Mauli in someone's home?  ·  Sketch 3 levels: who buys what, at what price, with what checks.

## Slide 16
7
CHIT 7
THE MISSING NUMBER
Followers and open rates don't prove love. Invent ONE new number that does.
YOUR TASK:  Name it, define it, say how Mauli would measure it — and the decision it changes.
START HERE
The founder doubts the numbers she has.  ·  Ask: what would people do if the brand disappeared tomorrow?  ·  Test: can Mauli measure it with things they already have (orders, emails, the quiz)? If not — wrong metric.

## Slide 17
8
CHIT 8
THE YOUNGER CROWD
Young people love the product when they try it but most don't know it exists. How do we reach them?
YOUR TASK:  Design the doorway in. No discounts. No ‘just post on TikTok.’
START HERE
The pack says under-35s “absolutely loved the product” when reached. So the product is fine — the doorway isn't.  ·  Ask: where does a 27-year-old already practise self-care?  ·  Entry products exist: £24–29 lip balm and aroma remedies.
