# Review of the supplied draft direction

This preserves the useful ambition while recording assumptions that the team should resolve. Original documents stay unchanged.

| Draft element | Evidence/interpretation issue | Execution adjustment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Always return 2–3 products, never a single item | This is the personal dossier's proposed rule, not a demonstrated customer need | Explain a ritual, offer a starter option and let customers choose basket size |
| AOV can rise from discounting alone | Discounting does not by itself raise net AOV; basket and order-mix changes can obscure interpretation | Define net AOV alongside margin, promotional share and contribution |
| Mandatory dosha or diagnostic experience | User preference and wellness education do not establish medical validity | Voluntary discovery, transparent mappings, reviewed wording and skip path |
| Inventory/margin reweights the 'mystic reading' | The PRD proposes commercially biased narratives | Use fit/constraints first and disclose recommendation logic; do not manufacture a wellness reason to clear stock |
| UHNW as the assumed audience | An aspirational PRD label is narrower than the supplied brand psychographics and not validated customer distribution | Test needs, purchase behaviour and budget before assigning an income segment |
| Heritage/diaspora customers assumed wealthy and uniformly motivated | Group averages and identity labels do not describe an individual customer's needs | Recruit on expressed interests and occasions; validate cultural relevance through interviews |
| General claim that in-chat checkout is retreating | Team notes describe a landscape inference; current Shopify help distinguishes channel-specific checkout behaviour | Record each channel's current supported journey and Mauli eligibility separately |
| Seven-person work allocation | Historical names/roles appear in the team notes; owner has specified a new lead remit | Confirm accountable owners and retain overlap where useful |

Sources: supplied `mauli-rituals-strategy-brief personal student written.md`, `agentic_ai_integration_prd.md` and `Mauli Rituals.md`; current [Shopify Agentic Storefronts help](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts), accessed 10 October 2026. Adjustments are project recommendations, not client-approved decisions.
