# Mauli Rituals: updated execution brief

Working recommendation · 10 October 2026 · independent pro bono student advisory work, Future of Commerce, Hult Business School. Instructor: Professor Nikhil Soi, as supplied by the project owner. Team and client validation pending.

This replaces the personal strategy brief as the active direction. It is a working manuscript, not the final typeset four-page submission. Evidence locations and unresolved decisions follow in the [ledger](decision-assumption-ledger.md).

## 1. Decision

**Build an incremental ritual-discovery campaign around Mauli's existing Shopify experience: pair a clearer, optional personalised recommendation journey with practitioner-led social/live education, then expand only where contribution, customer understanding and repeat behaviour justify investment.**

The strategic purpose is sustained relevance as discovery changes. The commercial question is whether Mauli can carry the meaning of its rituals from first encounter into confident choice and continued practice. Preserve the brand's sensory and human explanation while making product facts and guidance easier to understand. The Kautuka-inspired connecting thread is a proposed creative motif supporting that journey; it is not the business case by itself.

## 2. Diagnosis grounded in the supplied briefing

The classroom challenge materials describe an existing premium brand with D2C, retail and spa relationships, an on-site dosha quiz, and pressure around the economics of acquisition. They report £87 AOV, 61% D2C mix, 72% Meta/28% Google marketing allocation, 46% new/54% returning and 77% newsletter opens. These are founder-attributed classroom figures with unresolved periods and definitions. They do not establish current CAC, profitability, retention or channel incrementality. Team notes separately record “70/20” paid allocation: that discrepancy must be resolved before budget decisions. [S1–S3]

Three exposures warrant testing. First, an intermediary can reduce a sensory ritual to a product comparison while losing context and introducing incorrect claims. The team's exploratory AI audit reports inconsistent product information across single runs of ten questions; absent raw runs and repeated measurements, this is a warning to investigate rather than a stable visibility score. Second, existing quiz completion may not mean a shopper understands or trusts the recommendation. The journey teammate must identify actual friction before proposing replacement. Third, spa and practitioner encounters may offer trusted education but lack a measurable, partner-agreed continuation into an owned relationship. This is an opportunity hypothesis, not an established conversion leak. [S2, S4]

The immediate constraint is evidence and operating capacity. Neither a larger basket nor greater reach necessarily creates profit. A campaign must connect attention to a useful next step, account for delivery costs, and avoid implying that purchasing more products proves deeper ritual practice.

## 3. Priority audience and channel choices

Research both B2B and D2C, but concentrate the coursework demonstration on one D2C journey: a visitor seeking a manageable ritual and understandable product guidance. Segment by expressed need, category familiarity, sensory preference, available time, price considerations and desired evidence. A concern-led shopper may prefer direct product information; a ritual-seeking shopper may welcome optional dosha education. Neither should need a diagnostic label to browse. London or diaspora relevance is a research hypothesis to explore through voluntary accounts of heritage and experience, never an assumed trait or purchasing power. [S5]

For B2B, prioritise a candidate practitioner/spa partner with relevant treatments, staff capacity and willingness to support an attributable pilot. Qualification should cover location, business type, treatment volume, purchasing authority, training requirements, replenishment cycle, commercial terms and service cost. A qualified business agreeing a sample or pilot is B2B activation; a guest choosing home guidance is a separate D2C event. Do not combine wholesale sell-in with retail sell-through as Mauli revenue.

Map owned search/product pages, quiz, email, paid campaigns, social/live content and partner referrals before assigning spend. Select the first campaign entry point from that map and the available partner/content capacity. The supplied 60/40 brand/activation split remains a proposal requiring an economic rationale, not a binding allocation. [S2]

## 4. Two coordinated moves

**Move A — explainable personalisation.** Prototype a brief discovery flow using declared preferences and reviewed product information. Show a useful ritual, why it fits, relevant constraints, total product price and a clear next step. Permit a single-product starter, an optional fuller routine, a product-free practice and direct browsing. No mandatory multiproduct recommendation, forced account or email gate. Recommendation logic should be inspectable; a deterministic demonstration can test the interaction before an AI service is justified. Any later AI layer must preserve approved facts and reviewed wellness wording.

The proposed circular chord interaction can express connections between preferences, elements and ritual steps. Its explanatory design is still pending the owner's longer concept document. It must earn its place through comprehension and usability; retain a readable text equivalent and reduced-motion option. The separate splash/motion work can provide the presentation hook without becoming a prerequisite for the customer task. Optional voice guidance, including the proposed ElevenLabs concept, requires a separate value, cost and accuracy test. It is not required for the first prototype.

**Move B — social/live ritual education.** Develop one short practitioner/founder-style demonstration around a specific occasion or need, with a trackable link to the same discovery flow and optional follow-up guidance. Use an approved human presenter and reviewed explanations; establish recording and reuse permissions. A partner continuation card or QR can extend the same campaign where commercially agreed. Cultural context and practical instruction should remain visible when content is clipped or shared. Avoid manufactured testimonials, invented outcomes or guaranteed wellness effects.

Together these address two required coursework areas: AI personalisation and social/live commerce. Positioning centres on accessible, informed ritual practice; merchandising provides transparent starter and fuller options; pricing protects value through explicit total cost and measured promotion choices; customer experience joins discovery, choice and voluntary continuation. Structured product/FAQ content supports faithful AI discovery, but channel eligibility and integration must be checked before recommending a specific rollout.

## 5. Measurement and commercial test

Use AAARRR as an event map, with separate D2C person/order and B2B account/opportunity units. For the proposed D2C activation, record a shopper choosing or saving a ritual step after viewing its explanation; quiz completion alone is a process measure. Pair this with a short comprehension check in prototype testing. The three outcome priorities are contribution after acquisition, matured 90-day second-purchase conversion, and voluntary reported ritual practice. [S6]

Calculate contribution from net sales and retained shipping income after product, fulfilment, payment, return, commission and variable service costs, then acquisition spend. Show setup and fixed operating costs separately. Compare contribution-based cohort value with a consistently defined CAC; an observed 180-day value is not lifetime value. Only buyers with the full observation window enter the 90-day repeat denominator. Record purchases of additional ritual steps as commercial expansion, separate from self-reported use.

Track discoverability through a fixed, repeatable SEO/AEO/GEO question panel: inclusion, citation, factual accuracy, unsupported claims and attributable visits where observable. Preserve dates, prompts, outputs and scoring. Guardrails include abandonment, complaints, unsubscribes, customer understanding, staff burden and per-session service cost.

Before a live pilot, agree its cost ceiling, assignment method, duration, minimum useful effect and stop criteria. Compare the intervention with the existing journey using a credible holdout where feasible; otherwise label results observational. Forecast a no-recommendation baseline and downside/base/upside intervention scenarios using editable assumptions for traffic, conversion, net basket, repeat, cost and adoption. Include cannibalisation, rollout delay and incremental workload. No scenario is a promised uplift. Continue only when plausible contribution gains cover incremental costs within an agreed payback window and brand guardrails hold.

## 6. Delivery, exclusions and submission

Immediately: reconcile sources and KPI definitions, finish the journey audit, prioritise ICP hypotheses, produce one local prototype and prepare the campaign/test specification. A subsequent small pilot depends on client ownership, reviewed content, budget and analytics access. Longer-term options include improved knowledge assets and practitioner workflows only after demand and account economics are established. These are sequencing bands, not commitments.

Exclude a Shopify replacement, production recommendation infrastructure, compulsory voice, mandatory bundles, diagnostic health claims and a full therapist subscription platform from this coursework MVP. AR/VR is deferred because a credible interaction and distribution test already satisfy the chosen channel priorities. Avoid proliferating features before establishing customer value and operational feasibility.

The public project site will present an in-progress case study, with placeholders for seven team contacts and clearly dated work. Final results, feedback and grade remain pending. A custom splash will be supplied separately. Public documentation is distinct from deploying a client storefront.

The assessed output remains a maximum four-page brief plus appendices and a six-minute, five-to-eight-slide pitch followed by four-minute Q&A. Compress this manuscript into that format after team review; put detailed ICPs, metrics, evidence and financial sensitivities in appendices. [S1]
