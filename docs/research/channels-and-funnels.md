This addresses long-term relevance and adaptability, not a turnaround or wholesale platform replacement. The seven-person team can demonstrate a bounded marketing MVP; the user's remit covers metrics, AI and recommendation logic, alongside a teammate studying journey and technical shortcomings. These are **user-input scope decisions**, not client commitments.

## AAARRR convention and measurement rules

This repo uses **Awareness → Acquisition → Activation → Retention → Revenue → Referral**. This six-stage convention is a coursework operationalisation; customer behaviour can loop or occur out of order. Revenue may happen before measurable habit retention. Report both stage and actual timestamp; do not force a linear story onto customers.

**Unit:** D2C uses consent-permitted visitor/session for anonymous discovery and customer/order for purchase; B2B uses account/opportunity. A spa guest is a D2C person, not a B2B account. A wholesale order is Mauli sell-in revenue, while a retailer shopper's purchase is sell-through: never add both as Mauli direct sales.

### D2C proposed stage definitions

| Stage | Proposed qualifying event / numerator | Denominator or cohort | Guardrail / diagnostic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Exposed audience in each channel, separately | Valid channel reach; search impressions separate from social | Reach is not awareness recall; sampling studies needed for recall |
| Acquisition | First identifiable landing or first consented contact from source | Source impressions/clicks where reliable | Preserve unknown/direct; do not infer identity across devices |
| Activation | **Customer receives useful guidance and selects a feasible ritual step**, captured as result-view plus explicit step-select/save | Eligible quiz/guide starts; split new vs returning | Quiz completion alone is a weaker proxy; email opt-in and voice launch are not value activation |
| Retention | Return to guidance or self-reported repeat ritual use in defined follow-up window | Activated cohort eligible for that window | Repeat visit ≠ habit; separate voluntary use report from click proxy |
| Revenue | Paid order not cancelled; contribution calculated after variable costs | Activated cohort or all acquired cohort, both named | Return/refund maturity; discount and fulfilment cost; allow single-item choice |
| Referral | Referred recipient becomes acquired/activated or purchases | Eligible referrers / referred visitors, stated separately | Shares/UGC are intent or content outputs, not successful referral |

**Core activation variant:** for ordinary product browsing, meaningful activation may instead be “user can explain suitability and selects one intended product/step.” Test this in interviews; do not make the quiz mandatory. Post-purchase first ritual completion is a stronger downstream product activation event, measured separately and voluntarily. Product recommendation should explain why and allow budget/time constraints and no-purchase options, rather than imposing a multi-product basket.

### B2B proposed stage definitions

| Stage | Proposed event | Denominator / ownership | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Named suitable account reached through introduction, event or partner content | Target-account list | Count accounts rather than every contact |
| Acquisition | Qualified enquiry: business type, fit, decision role, location, expected need | Accounts reached / enquiries received | Enquiry is not contractual demand |
| Activation | **Partner completes staff training and executes first agreed customer-facing pilot ritual or stocked-product demonstration** | Qualified pilot accounts | Sample shipment or contract signature alone is setup, not realised value |
| Retention | Active programme persists; trained staff remain; repeat reorder when due | Activated partner cohort, cadence adjusted | One-off corporate gifting needs repeat-occasion logic, not monthly churn |
| Revenue | Recognised net wholesale/programme revenue and contribution | Active partner accounts | Training, samples, support, discounts, logistics and credit costs included |
| Referral | Partner introduces another qualified account; or guest follows agreed owned handoff | Account referrals and guest handoffs measured separately | Avoid conflating guest acquisitions with business leads |

Retail activation can be store staff trained + first documented consumer sale; spa activation is therapist trained + first delivered ritual; corporate gifting activation is approved gifts delivered for the agreed occasion with recipient experience verified. Select subtype before calculating a blended rate.

## Proposed cross-channel handoff designs

1. Concern article / paid creative → existing Shopify collection → optional guide/quiz → useful result → optional saved ritual → existing checkout. Instrument source, collection, guide version and recommendation ID; do not store unrestricted conversation transcripts as routine analytics.
2. Partner treatment → opt-in continuation card → partner-specific landing → relevant home ritual → optional owned contact. Partner receives agreed aggregate reporting; a QR scan is acquisition, selected home step is activation, purchase is revenue.
3. Creator/live demonstration → campaign landing → guide or direct PDP choice. Hold creative/campaign source separate from recommendation variant to identify where any uplift arises.
4. AI answer / voice prompt → source-supported explanation → text/PDP → optional guidance. Record available referring domain; an assistant may conceal/refuse attribution, so “direct” cannot automatically become GEO success.

## Test sequencing and horizons (proposals, not forecasts)

| Horizon | Practical deliverable | Evidence gate |
|---|---|---|
| Immediate coursework | Baseline journey map; event dictionary; clickable quiz/chord/thread and optional voice demo on fixture products | Team verifies one complete path, source fidelity, useful guidance, budget/time choice |
| Short term: proposed 4–8 week client pilot | One collection + one eligible existing channel; partner handoff only if approved | Obtain baseline and sample-size calculation; proceed if contribution and useful activation improve without trust/support harm |
| Long term: proposed 6–18 month learning horizon | Extend only validated guidance, structured product answers and partner playbooks | Repeat cohort evidence; delivery capacity; maintenance owner; positive incremental economics |

No numerical target or paid-channel allocation is defensible before baselines. Run a holdout or controlled split where feasible; predefine primary measure, observation window and exclusions. Compare recommendation exposure cohorts while correcting for selection; quiz takers can already be more motivated. Academic demo success is not commercial uplift evidence.
