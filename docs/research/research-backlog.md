## Review protocol

Every claim carries a URL or original-file locator, source date/access date, status and limitations. Every experiment carries a segment, entry point, activation event, primary economic outcome, guardrails, cost assumptions and an owner. Team decisions supersede drafts through a decision record, not by editing original inputs.
