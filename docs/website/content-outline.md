## The problem

Working headline: **How does a deeply personal ritual travel through an increasingly mediated purchase?**

Explain the commercial tension in ordinary language: shoppers may encounter the brand through search, a recommendation, a partner treatment, social content or a product page. The project asks what information and guidance should connect those moments so customers can choose with confidence and return when the product is useful to them.

Use no more than three evidence statements, each with an expandable source note: existing channel complexity; reported acquisition economics; and the need to carry sensory/heritage context. Label briefing figures as reported and keep their missing periods/denominators visible. Omit large numerical claims when the caveat would overwhelm the point.

## Marketing insights

Use four editorial spreads or rows, each with an observation, why it matters and a proposed response:

1. Different entry points need different next steps. A business sample/pilot and a consumer's saved ritual are distinct activation events.
2. Help should feel useful before it asks for commitment. Explain the recommendation and permit simpler alternatives.
3. A purchase and a practice tell different stories. Measure repeat contribution and voluntary reported use separately.
4. Discovery needs accurate source material. Measure AI answer quality and citations separately from attributable visits and sales.

The ICP material should show jobs, triggers and constraints, with explicit hypothesis labels. Avoid turning speculative personas into portraits of real customers.
