# Mauli public asset reference archive

Captured 10 October 2026 for independent student brand research. See [brandbook](../../docs/brand/brandbook.md), [tokens](tokens.json) and [manifest](asset-manifest.json).

The manifest distinguishes downloaded HTTP response bytes, extracted inline SVG DOM markup, and discovered-only assets. SHA-256 and byte lengths identify the exact saved rendition. Source URLs retain CDN version/size parameters. These files are research references; they are not an official brand kit or all files on the Mauli CDN.

- `images/`: public imagery and wordmarks from the homepage inventory and linked serum product, skincare collection and story page source references. Multiple image-size variants may exist.
- `fonts/`: discovered font representations, including Adobe-hosted faces and Shopify/app declarations. Declaration does not prove active rendered use. See [font face evidence](../../docs/brand/evidence/font-faces.json).
- `svg/`: unique homepage inline SVGs, including third-party UI, social and payment symbols. DOM serialization is identified separately from HTTP originals.
- `css/`: original observed brand/theme stylesheets and Adobe kit declarations, with copyright/EULA references preserved.

**Third-party rights remain with their owners.** No blanket license applies. Font embedding and asset reuse rights for this project's domain have not been established; do not infer them from public delivery. The showcase can use the audit's observations without automatically shipping these files. Video URLs and unrelated app CSS are discovered-only; the quiz interior returned HTTP 429 and is outside verified coverage.
