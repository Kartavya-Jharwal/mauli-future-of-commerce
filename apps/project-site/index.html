02 / The question

How does a deeply personal ritual travel through an increasingly mediated purchase?

A shopper might arrive through a search answer, a partner treatment, social content or a product page. Each encounter carries a different level of knowledge, trust and intent.

Our question is how those moments can connect: helping people choose with confidence, carry a treatment into everyday life and return when the ritual remains useful.

The ambition: long-term relevance and adaptability, built through incremental marketing and guidance.

04 / Marketing insights

One brand.
Different reasons to enter.

These are customer hypotheses to test, not verified descriptions of Mauli’s customer base. Cultural gifting is an occasion; practical routine building belongs within ritual continuation.

I

Cultural
reconnection

“Help me make a meaningful connection my own.”

An abroad-born South Asian customer may seek accessible cultural context without assumptions about their knowledge or identity. An engaged gift buyer may share this motivation.

Proposed entry
Brand story, cultural editorial or a meaningful gift occasion.
Proposed activation
Choose and save a ritual with context they understand.
II

Spa to
everyday ritual

“Help me continue what felt good.”

A treatment guest needs a feasible home routine: a clear sequence, realistic time commitment and continuity with their experience.

Proposed entry
Therapist guidance, partner referral or a post-treatment follow-up.
Proposed activation
Start a manageable routine and voluntarily report practice.
III

Premium care.
Personal or professional.

“Give me confidence in the fit.”

Concern-led premium consumers and luxury partners share a need for credible guidance. Their buying processes stay distinct: personal product fit versus training, margin and a viable partnership.

Consumer activation
Understand an explained product recommendation.
B2B activation
Qualify a sample, training session or pilot with success criteria.

05 / Prototyping

Guidance with
a connecting thread.

A proposed experience. Customer testing is still ahead.

Original explanatory sketch / not a product interface

Make the recommendation understandable.

Reimagine wellness discovery around preferences, routines and explanations. The chord concept is intended to show how a recommendation connects to what a person has shared.

An optional voice introduction could support first interaction. The pilot must establish whether it helps more than a straightforward guided flow.

Wellness discovery only. No diagnosis or clinical assessment.

The next validation question

Can a person explain why this suggestion fits, and find a simpler alternative, without additional assistance? Compare the chord explanation with a clear text version.

07 / Recommendations

Three horizons.
A measured progression.

Working recommendations, subject to client review and baseline validation.

Immediate / establish

Make the next step clear.

Clarify product and ritual guidance. Define consumer and partner activation events, reconcile source metrics and agree the measurement baseline.

Measure: explained choice, activation, data completeness.
Short term / learn

Pilot useful guidance.

Pair one explainable discovery flow with educational social or live content. Scope the pilot around existing Shopify capabilities and an agreed cost ceiling.

Measure: completion, contribution, repeat purchase and voluntary practice.
Long term / adapt

Expand what earns its place.

Improve structured discovery content and partner continuity. Expand only when customer usefulness and contribution economics support the next investment.

Measure: incremental contribution, retention and discovery accuracy.
