Future of Commerce · Independent student case study

A thread through
the future of commerce.

Carrying a personal ritual through an increasingly mediated purchase, for Mauli Rituals.

01 — The problem

How does a ritual stay personal when the purchase is not?

Shoppers arrive through search answers, partner treatments, social clips and product pages, each with a different level of knowledge and trust. The work asks how those moments can connect without a rebuild for its own sake.

02 — Approach

Four passes.

  1. 01

    Interpret & audit

    Read the brief, the store and the brand as a shopper would.

  2. 02

    Customer jobs

    Map what people are trying to get done at each encounter.

  3. 03

    Economics

    Model contribution, cost ceilings and what to measure first.

  4. 04

    Prototype

    Sketch one explainable guidance flow worth testing.

07 — What we would sharpen

Three horizons.

  1. Immediate

    Make the next step clear.

    Clarify guidance, define activation events, agree the baseline.

  2. Short term

    Pilot useful guidance.

    One explainable discovery flow, within Shopify and a cost ceiling.

  3. Longer term

    Expand what earns its place.

    Invest further only when usefulness and economics support it.
