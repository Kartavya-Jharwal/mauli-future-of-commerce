01

The core problem

How does a deeply personal ritual travel through an increasingly mediated purchase?

A shopper may arrive through a search answer, a partner treatment, social content or a product page. Each encounter carries a different level of knowledge, trust and intent.

Our question is how those moments can connect: helping people choose with confidence, carry a treatment into everyday life and return when the ritual remains useful.

Long-term relevance and adaptability — built through incremental marketing and guidance, not a rebuild for its own sake.
